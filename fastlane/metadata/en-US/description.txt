Interview My Kid is a private, on-device app for recording short video interviews with your child. Use it after a baseball game, before school, at the end of a hard day, or for any moment worth keeping.

Pick a topic. The app generates seven warm, age-appropriate cue cards using on-device Apple Intelligence (iOS 26+) or your own OpenRouter key. If neither is available, a curated bank of over 360 hand-written questions is always there. Edit the cards, save sets you love for next time, then start recording.

The cue card sits on YOUR screen as a teleprompter — read each question aloud while the camera captures your kid's answer. Tap Next to move to the next card. The recorded video is clean by default; the prompts only appear on your screen.

When you're done, the session is saved with the questions you used, in a private library on your device. Replay it later, save sets to reuse, or export to Photos with the questions baked in so the answers always have context.

CATEGORIES

• Sports & Performance — pre-game, post-game, right after a play, season recap
• School — before school, after school
• Daily Life — end-of-day check-in
• Special Moments — big feeling, milestone, after a trip
• Free-form — open-ended for anything else

FEATURES

• AI-generated cue cards across 11 categories with hand-curated fallbacks for each
• On-device Apple Intelligence support — no internet, no API key needed (iOS 26+ on supported iPhones)
• Cloud generation via your own OpenRouter API key for top-quality questions
• Built-in question bank (360+ hand-written prompts) — always works, even fully offline
• Save and reuse cue card sets — never regenerate the same set twice
• Drag-to-reorder cue cards in the editor
• Teleprompter overlay during recording — choose top, middle, or bottom position
• Optional burn-in: bake question text into the exported video for sharing
• Six color themes including two light modes
• 100% local storage — your videos never leave your phone

PRIVACY

Interview My Kid stores everything on your device. The app does not collect, transmit, or share your videos, prompts, or personal information. The only outbound network traffic is to OpenRouter (only when you choose Cloud generation) using an API key you provide.
