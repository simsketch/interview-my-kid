Interview My Kid
iPhone & iPad · Free

The interview questions you’ll wish you asked

Turn everyday moments with your kid into treasured memories. The app writes the questions — you just hit record.

Works offline. Everything stays on your device.

How it works

1

Pick a moment

After a game, before school, at the end of a hard day. Eleven categories, from sports to milestones to free-form.

2

Get seven cue cards

The app writes warm, age-appropriate questions using on-device Apple Intelligence. Edit them, reorder them, save the sets you love.

3

Read and record

The card sits on your screen like a teleprompter. Read it aloud, tap Next, and the camera keeps rolling on your kid.

Everything in the app

  • AI cue cards across 11 categories, each with hand-curated fallbacks
  • On-device Apple Intelligence — works with no internet and no API key (iOS 26+)
  • Optional cloud generation with your own OpenRouter key
  • 360+ hand-written questions built in
  • Save and reuse cue card sets
  • Drag to reorder cards in the editor
  • Teleprompter overlay at top, middle, or bottom
  • Burn question text into exported video for sharing
  • Multiple kid profiles
  • Six color themes, including two light modes
  • Export to Photos with questions baked in
  • Local storage for every recording
